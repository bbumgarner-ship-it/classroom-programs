Speed Clicker

Explore five worlds from behind your character. Train speed, race their champions, hatch a following pet, and find the world portal.

W/A/S/D or arrow keys move. E interacts with a nearby boss, upgrade stand, or portal. Clicking only trains speed — it does not make you jog.

Your browser needs canvas to show the world. All training, race, portal, and save buttons are available below.

W/A/S/D to explore · E to interact


World challengers

Meet them in the world and press E, or start a race here. During a race your character runs automatically; keep training to gain an edge.

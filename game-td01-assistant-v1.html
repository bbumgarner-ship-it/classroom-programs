Snowbound Towers

Move with the arrow keys (or the on-screen arrows). Walk close to a tree or animal to use your axe. Click to choose a target, or swing at the nearest one. Press Space to swing. Press W to collect nearby fallen wood and meat and earn money. Animals can hurt you once you build your first tower.

Buy stronger towers, then click an open snowy spot. Towers automatically defend you from animals.
